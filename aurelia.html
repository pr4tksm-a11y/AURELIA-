the six pillars

One practice at a time.

Each pillar is a module in the arc. You never work on all six at once — the method introduces one, lets it settle, then layers the next.

01 — hair

Cut for your face, color for your tone

A stylist's honest opinion beats a guess. Then: clean roots, sulfate-free wash days, and heat with a strategy — upside-down for volume, diffuser for waves, round brush for the blown-out look that lasts days.

  • volume
  • dry shampoo
  • silk protection
  • cut & color consult
02 — skin & body

Science, softly

SPF every single day. Hydration for oily skin — stripping only invites rebound oil. Vitamin C for old marks. Moisturize your body within three minutes of the shower, while it's still damp. And a dermatologist for anything prescription-strength.

  • SPF daily
  • AM / PM routine
  • vitamin C
  • derm referrals
03 — face

Frame it gently

Brows shaped or microbladed — minimal effort, maximal structure. Lash serums and subtle enhancements exist, and they're optional, never required. Blotting papers live in the bag if shine is a guest that overstays.

  • brows
  • lashes
  • optional enhancements
04 — makeup

Less, but placed

A free counter consult beats a drawer of guesses. Liner, mascara, a whisper of foundation and bronzer. But none of it can save what skincare neglected — skin first, always.

  • free consults
  • five-minute face
  • skin first
05 — wardrobe

A uniform beats a wardrobe

Quality fabric over logos, fit over fashion. The black-turtleneck-and-dark-denim effect: one formula, bought well, worn on repeat. Boots in winter, wedges in summer — and visibly uncomfortable is the only bad look.

  • your formula
  • fit guides
  • seasonal shoes
06 — health

The part that makes the rest work

Fuel, never restriction — diet pills and crash diets always collect their debt. Strength three times a week, yoga for flexibility, meditation morning and night. Goals beyond the mirror. Learn a skill; competence is the deepest confidence there is.

  • strength
  • sleep
  • meditation
  • goals beyond the mirror

the weekly rhythm

Your week, by design.

Open any day to see the room, the time, and what you'll need. This is the default rhythm — your assessment calibrates it to you.

the room

Bathroom, door shut, playlist on. Warm water — never hot. A full minute of scalp massage before anything else.

you'll need

  • sulfate-free shampoo
  • dry shampoo for days two & three
  • leave-in, if you're wavy or curly

A clean scalp is the foundation; everything after is protection.

the room

Anywhere with floor space. Dumbbells if you have them, bodyweight if you don't. Protein within an hour after — if it suits your age and body.

you'll need

  • dumbbells or bodyweight
  • protein shake or a real meal
  • water, nearby

Muscle is the frame the glow hangs on.

the room

Anywhere the phone isn't. Ten minutes in the morning, ten at night. Box breathing, a body scan, or simply sitting with the lights low.

you'll need

  • nothing — that's the point

Meditation is the cheapest skin treatment there is.

the room

Bathroom, evening, low light. Actives and masks go here — patch-tested products only, and never stacked with Proactiv-style systems. Prescription-strength actives (tretinoin and friends) belong to a dermatologist's office, introduced slowly.

you'll need

  • patch-tested mask or active
  • a derm's sign-off for anything prescription
  • patience — start slow

Skin repairs on a schedule; give it the good stuff weekly.

the room

Bedroom mirror, music on. Heat protectant first — then round brush if you're straight, diffuser on low if you're wavy or curly. Body lotion on damp skin, three minutes out of the shower.

you'll need

  • heat protectant
  • round brush or diffuser
  • body lotion

Effort looks effortless when it's scheduled.

the room

The long way round. A yoga class, a slow hour on the mat, or a long walk with nowhere to be. Sunscreen — yes, even in winter.

you'll need

  • yoga mat or walking shoes
  • SPF 30+
  • no headphones, occasionally

Flexibility and long walks are the anti-HIIT half of the method.

the room

Kitchen table, tea going cold. Nails done — gel, once a month, forgotten about. Outfits planned. And your goals list reviewed: the one about your life, not just your reflection.

you'll need

  • nail kit or salon booking
  • your goals list — beyond the mirror
  • next week's three wins, written down

A Sunday hour buys a serene week.
